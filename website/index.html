The Complete Command Matrix

Arena provides 9 highly specialized commands giving you complete granular control over every phase of clipping.

Command Description Example Use Case
arena init Interactive setup wizard to initialize workflows and clip duration preferences. arena init
arena process All-in-one processing pipeline. Runs transcribing, AI filtering, sentence alignment, and clip exports. arena process video.mp4 --fast
arena transcribe Extracts video audio, enhances speech quality (noise-reduction), and exports JSON transcript. arena transcribe podcast.mov -o speech.json
arena analyze Run AI & audio energy analysis and generate metadata clips JSON without exporting videos. arena analyze talk.mp4 -n 8
arena generate Renders physical video clips instantly using an existing analysis JSON metadata. arena generate lecture.mp4 analysis.json --select 1,3
arena format Auto-format aspects ratios and padding styles of clips for platforms (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, etc.). arena format clips/ -p tiktok -o social/
arena detect-scenes Analyzes and outputs visual scene change boundaries using pixel difference calculations. arena detect-scenes raw.mp4 --threshold 0.35
arena config Reads, sets, or resets configuration parameters like OpenAI API keys and thresholds. arena config set openai_api_key "sk-..."
arena extract-audio Quickly strip audio streams to high-quality MP3/WAV with custom bitrate configurations. arena extract-audio talk.mov --mono --bitrate 128k

#behind-the-code

"I got tired of paid web-based video clippers charging $30/month per seat."

As a developer and creator, I wanted a fast way to turn podcast recordings and long talk videos into vertical clips for social feeds. But every modern tool I tried followed the same frustrating pattern:

They forced me to upload massive multi-gigabyte raw files to their cloud, made me wait in slow remote queues, hit me with strict monthly duration limits, and charged hefty recurring fees—all for what is essentially transcript matching and FFmpeg rendering.

Worse, as a developer, I had zero programmatic control over the cuts or output directories. My raw content had to sit on some third-party server.

So, I took ownership and built Arena.

It is a 100% local, developer-first clipping pipeline. It transcribes, ranks the best hooks using a 4-layer AI agent model, aligns natural thought boundaries, and formats beautiful mobile Crops. It is free, completely open-source, runs in milliseconds with lossless seek copying, and respects your privacy by running fully offline on your own OpenAI key.

Built for creators who value control, speed, and privacy.

Absolutely not. Arena is designed for 100% local operation. Your video files, audio streams, transcript databases, and final clipped exports stay entirely on your disk. Only text transcripts are sent to OpenAI for candidate ranking, ensuring your pre-release content remains fully secure and confidential.

Arena relies on a lightweight stack: Node.js (v18+) to drive the interactive TypeScript CLI, Python (v3.10+) to orchestrate speech-to-text algorithms, and ffmpeg to process fast video operations. The CLI includes a pre-flight checker to ensure everything is configured during setup.

By enabling the --fast flag, Arena avoids expensive video re-encoding. Instead, it instructs ffmpeg to seek directly to natural sentence/scene boundaries and copy the raw container streams losslessly. This exports clips in milliseconds rather than minutes, with zero generational quality loss.
